# Code of conduct

Canvasly follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work.

**Welcome:** empathy and respect for differing views, helpful and constructive feedback, owning and learning from mistakes.

**Not acceptable:** harassment of any kind, personal or political attacks, sexualized language or imagery, and publishing
someone's private information without their permission.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behavior that breaks this code, please contact the maintainer through the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/AnasSAV). Reports are handled privately. People who break this code may be asked to change
their behavior, have their contributions removed, or be blocked from the project.
